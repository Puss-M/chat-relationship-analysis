# Framework

## Main Labels

Choose exactly one main label:

- `学习搭子`: shared learning, collaboration, frequent exchange, trust is real but romance is weak or absent
- `高信任朋友`: warmth, disclosure, and mutual regard are strong, but the relationship is still framed as friendship
- `礼貌合作`: interaction is functional, cordial, and low-risk, but not deeply personal
- `单向情绪承接`: one side repeatedly absorbs emotion, reassures, or stabilizes the other more than is returned
- `模糊暧昧`: attraction or pair-bond cues exist, but commitment and real-world progression remain unclear
- `明显推进`: repeated evidence of special dyadic interest, concrete progression, and stronger-than-friend framing

## Secondary Modifiers

Use up to two:

- `高欣赏`
- `现实接触增多`
- `资源交换双向`
- `轻度失衡`
- `情绪依赖迹象`

## Scorecard

Score each dimension from 0 to 5.

### 1. 双向主动性

- `0-1`: one side carries nearly all initiation
- `2`: some return initiative, but inconsistent
- `3`: reasonably shared
- `4`: clearly mutual
- `5`: strongly mutual, with active follow-up from both sides

### 2. 情绪支持对等度

- `0-1`: support is mostly one-way
- `2`: limited reciprocity
- `3`: moderate mutual support
- `4`: clearly reciprocal
- `5`: deep, repeated, two-way emotional presence

### 3. 现实推进度

- `0-1`: almost entirely text-bound
- `2`: light references to meeting or future contact
- `3`: some concrete offline or future-oriented movement
- `4`: repeated real-world progression
- `5`: strong and consistent pair-oriented progression

### 4. 边界跨越度

- `0-1`: fully ordinary or formal
- `2`: warm but still conventional
- `3`: clear soft-boundary crossing, playful or special framing
- `4`: repeated romantic or exclusive cues
- `5`: explicit pair framing or strong romantic signaling

### 5. 交换失衡风险

- `0-1`: low risk, exchange is balanced
- `2`: mild asymmetry
- `3`: noticeable asymmetry
- `4`: strong one-sided use or dependence
- `5`: severe extraction pattern

## Theory Mapping

## Sternberg

Map evidence to:

- `亲密`: trust, disclosure, mutual understanding, emotional openness
- `激情`: flirtation, attraction cues, exclusivity undertones, special desire for dyadic attention
- `承诺`: stable future orientation, pair priority, repeated intent to maintain the bond

Important constraint:

- high intimacy alone is not enough to call it romance
- admiration alone is not enough to call it passion
- future references alone are not enough unless they imply pair priority

## Social Exchange

Map evidence to:

- help given vs received
- emotional support given vs received
- who invests attention and follow-up
- whether the user is being used as a task, validation, or regulation resource

## Output Template

### 速判

One sentence. Example:

`这段关系当前更像高信任的学习搭子，而不是已经进入明确恋爱推进阶段。`

### 关系标签

- 主标签: `...`
- 副标签: `...`

### 理论映射

Example:

`从三角理论看，亲密偏高、激情偏弱、承诺偏低；从交换理论看，整体互动双向，但情绪承接略向你倾斜。`

### 评分卡

- 双向主动性: `x/5`
- 情绪支持对等度: `x/5`
- 现实推进度: `x/5`
- 边界跨越度: `x/5`
- 交换失衡风险: `x/5`

### 证据摘录

Use this format:

- `[time] speaker: snippet` -> why it matters

### 建议动作

Allowed tone:

- `继续观察`
- `降低情绪投入`
- `用一次轻邀约测试现实推进`
- `不要把欣赏误判成承诺`

Disallowed tone:

- coercive or manipulative tactics
- certainty claims with weak evidence
- diagnostic or therapeutic language
